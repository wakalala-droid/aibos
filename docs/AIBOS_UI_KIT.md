# AI-BOS UI kit

The AIBOS x Mercury look, as one pack. Every page, notice, reminder, alert, toast, email and PDF is built from these pieces. Never a stock or library look.

Import: `import { Notice, BentoCard, NoticeArt, BrandMark, DocHeader } from '@/components/kit';`

## Brand elements
- **The mark** (`BrandMark`): white glyph on dark, navy on light. Files in `public/brand/`.
- **The splash art strip** (`NoticeArt`, `.notice-art-strip`): hairline tiles, the rising cyan money line with soft shading, the AIBOS mark in a white disc inside a dashed ring in the middle, a cyan point on the ring, and the rising double arrow at the top right in ink.
- **The document header** (`DocHeader`, `.doc-head`): the letterhead every document opens with: reminders, statements, notices and PDFs (owner, 18 Oct 2026). An ink band (`--band`, `#1c1917`, the same ink as the If unpaid blocks, never a deeper black) with a clear blue light in the top-left corner (the corner wash every card has, strong enough to see on the ink), painted in solid colours on the band itself (`color-mix` into `--band`), never a see-through gradient: that prints as a soft mask, which many PDF viewers drop, ending on the logo's gold rule (`--gold` at 70%, the rule in `aibos-lockup.png`). Top left: the white glyph, the white wordmark cropped to its bounds (`aibos-wordmark-white-tight.png`), a short gold rule and "Business operating system" in spaced capitals. Bottom left: the document name (22px, white) and a brand-cyan tag. At the back, one layer across the whole band and behind the words: three faint hairlines and the cyan trend line with its shading fading down. In front on the right, the emblem: the navy mark on a white disc inside a dashed ring and a fainter outer ring, the cyan point on the dashed ring and the white rising double arrow. No tiles. The emblem is always in front at full strength and the words keep clear of it (`--doc-art-room`): never faded, never tucked behind the name or the tag (owner, 18 Oct 2026). When the header is narrower than 680px (a container query on `.doc-head`, so it holds on a phone and beside the side menu alike) the emblem shrinks to 132px on the right, still in front at full strength, and the gold rule and the line hide. Under the band, the facts row: 12px spaced-capital labels and 15px values on one line each, two columns when the header is narrower than 680px.
  `<DocHeader name="Payment reminder" tag="Past due" facts={[{ label: 'Prepared for', value: 'Dunslim Apartments' }, { label: 'Date', value: '18 October 2026' }, { label: 'From', value: 'AI-BOS' }]} />`
- **The corner wash**: one faint light from the top-left corner of every card (`--card-wash`).
- **Emails** use `public/brand/email-art.png` (the same strip as an image) and the grey mark in the footer. **PDFs** open with the same document header drawn in reportlab (`_letterhead` in `aibos-api/pdfdoc.py`, for `render()` and `report()`) and put the grey mark beside the footer words on every page. Every document, on screen or printed, ends with the grey mark and "Generated by AIBOS" in grey; a printed document carries it at the foot of every page, at the left margin, as `_footer` draws it (owner, 18 Oct 2026).

## Rules
- Geist only. Mercury-standard sizes (owner, 7 Oct 2026): body 15px, data and labels 14px, spaced capitals 12px, card titles 17px, page titles 22px. Taps at least 44px. Contrast at least 4.5:1.
- Numbers are ink. Red only for a figure that went the wrong way (`trendTone`, `signTone`).
- A month still running is never set against a whole one (audit 19 Nov 2026): sales are compared at their pace to the month end from the 7th, costs and profit once the month is over, and it is never the worst month (`fairMonth`, `isUnfinishedMonth` in `lib/change.ts`).
- Every panel over the page (a sheet, Talk, a welcome) takes focus in, keeps Tab inside and gives focus back on close (`hooks/useModalFocus.ts`). Every field has a label tied to it (`htmlFor` and `id`, with the row's id in a list). Every page names its browser tab (`titleFor` in `lib/nav.ts`).
- Charts use `--chart-1..6` and `--chart-muted`.
- No em-dashes, no comma before "and", no emoji. Plain owner words.
- No entrance animation on page content; icons move on hover only. No blurred sticky headers. (The public website has one scoped exception, below, and the app has two: the team scene and talking to AIBOS.)
- Home asks for one thing at a time (owner, 17 Nov 2026): five blocks (greeting with Record and Talk, Needs you, the AIBOS team scene edge to edge, the money, Today; on plans without the team, the sleeping team comes last), at most one nudge on the whole screen (the layout's plan notice and install card share the slot with Home's, `.one-nudge`; on a phone a promo nudge is its title and buttons), and Needs you is the only to-do list in the app, one line when nothing needs the owner. A new signal joins Needs you; it never becomes another card, and a figure elsewhere on Home never says something needs the owner that Needs you does not. The phone toolbar is one row; the business name sits in the top bar. On a phone the money comes before the team (`.home-team.is-first` is reordered in globals.css): the first look is the figure, not the stage. The nudge order is welcome back (`WelcomeBack`, after three days without an entry: catch up, or start from today), the first-week goal, the brief's ending, then Teach AIBOS: the short-lived ones first. Under the greeting, once the first week is done, the week's rhythm (`BooksRhythm`): seven dots, ink for a day the books were kept, today ringed in grey until something is recorded, and "Books kept 5 of the last 7 days": a count of the week, never a streak that one closed day breaks (audit 19 Nov 2026). There is no Simple or Pro switch (removed 17 Nov 2026): every owner sees the same app, the reports folded in the menu. The morning brief's one thing opens free for 14 days on every plan from the third recording day (`lib/briefTrial.ts`), then asks once (`BriefTrialEnded`). On a plan without the team the asleep team shows what it found in the owner's books just now (GET /auto/preview, counts only), never a description alone.

## Website navy (the public website only)
The owner, 10 Oct 2026: "too much white going on, add dynamic dark navy blue like on the AI section on most of the site". The app keeps its own light and black themes; the website is navy.
- The website root (and the sign-in page) carries `data-navy` and `data-theme="dark"`; `app/navy.css` turns the kit's dark tokens into the navy: page `#050b18`, cards `#0b1629`, blue-tinted hairlines and greys (text-3 8.5:1 and text-4 7.0:1 on cards). Every kit piece arrives in navy with no one-off class.
- **The product stays light.** The hero video, the feature theatre's scenes, the morning brief and the last Start free card are the light app floating on navy: give the block `data-theme="light"` (Rise takes `theme="light"`). White appears only as the product, never as the page.
- **The sky** (`components/brand/NavyPage.tsx`, `.navy-sky`): ONE fixed layer with three soft lights (blue, cyan, indigo) painted once, half a screen taller than the screen, sliding up as you scroll where the browser supports scroll-driven animation. Nothing moves while the page is still. The first sky (three huge lights drifting on their own clocks) doubled the slow frames on a PC and caused a third of them on a phone (measured 10 Oct 2026): never animate large layers on a clock.
- **Speed rules:** shadows of 40px blur or less (navy tokens shorten the kit's), no chart library on the website (the forecast is plain SVG). The website never loads the app's code (`AppShell` renders `AppFrame` in its own chunk for app routes only).
- Legal pages read on a light panel (`.mkt-paper` with `data-theme="light"`). The white mark and wordmark sit on navy. The phone's browser bar is navy.

## Website motion (the public website only)
The app stays still. The public website (`app/(marketing)`) may move, but only with the kit's own movements and timings from `globals.css`, so it feels like the app arriving:
- **Hero:** never animates. Its text is the largest paint on a phone and animating it delays it. The video does the moving.
- **Blocks coming into view, once (starting just before they reach the screen):** 12px over 200ms, like a notice (`update-in`); grids one card after another (`components/marketing/Rise.tsx`). Anything on screen at load just shows.
- **Lines:** the art strip's money line draws itself over 1.1s, like What's new (`whatsnew-draw`). Report bars and meters fill to their figure.
- **Notices:** the morning brief arrives like the update notice; a reminder slides in 24px from the right (`reminder-in`).
- **Hero video:** `components/marketing/HeroVideo.tsx` plays a video of the kit in action. It is made from these same kit pieces in `aibos-film` (`npm run render:hero`), with a laptop cut and a phone cut. It plays on its own, muted and looping, with no buttons (owner, 9 Oct 2026). MP4 (H.264) only, never WebM first (Edge on Windows froze on it), and written as plain HTML so the browser starts it before any script loads.
- **Feature theatre** (`components/marketing/FeatureTheatre.tsx`, scenes in `components/marketing/theatre/`): payroll, getting paid, deadlines, stock and Rooms & Stays, each a scene of about seven seconds built from kit pieces with the product's real figures (the payslip comes from `aibos-api/payroll.py`). A beat clock flips `is-on` on a few beats and CSS moves the pieces in kit timing. It plays only while a third of it is on screen, moves on by itself with a 2px line under the tab, pauses under a resting mouse and stops when a tab is chosen.
- **Pain to promise:** the brand line strikes each old job through and the answer rises in its place. **The goal:** four large lines light up once each reaches the middle of the screen.
- Only the video and the theatre loop, nothing floats over the content and nothing blocks the page. With `prefers-reduced-motion` none of the page motion runs (the sky is still and the scenes show their finished picture); the hero video still plays.
- Phones: everything fits at 320px. Long words wrap, never cut off; card contents use the full width under the icon and title.

## The team scene and the sphere (the app's two moving pieces)
The owner, 17 Nov 2026: "maintain the very same AIBOS UI kit for the design, but we just need some animation going on." Two pieces may move, and only when something is happening, so the movement tells the owner something:
- **The team scene:** light runs along a worker's line into the middle when it has found something, and out along every line while AIBOS auto is on; the dashed ring and the middle orbit turn while auto is on; a badge that needs the owner swells gently; a sweep crosses the badges while the team looks (Check now); a new line of live activity slides in (12px over 200ms, like a notice). A team at rest is still.
- **The sphere:** the water runs fast and the bars follow a voice while AIBOS speaks, the water jumps each time a word is heard while it listens, it swirls slowly with the gold light brighter while it thinks. Drawn on one canvas. Small on a button it is a still picture.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion` neither moves: the scene is still and the sphere is one picture.
